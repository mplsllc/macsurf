## How the score works

Each load is scored out of 100 from the diagnostic log — no subjective judgement:

| Weight | What it measures | From |
|---:|---|---|
| 40 | Content actually laid out **and painted** | `SITE boxes/text` + `bw_redraw plot_text` |
| 20 | Stylesheets applied vs dropped | `SITE css_ok/css_skip` |
| 15 | Images decoded ok | `SITE img_ok/img_fail` |
| 15 | Load time | `[+Nus]` first-paint timing |
| 10 | No JavaScript errors | `js err` lines |

Grades: 🟢 A/B (85+/70+) · 🟡 C (50+) · 🟠 D (30+) · 🔴 E (1+) · ⚫ F (can't load yet). Login: ✅ works · ⚠️ partial · ❌ no · — untested.
