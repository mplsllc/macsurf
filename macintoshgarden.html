Netscape 3.x Login Now Working
Published 2026 Apr 11 - 9:30am | Discuss this news post

As part of our effort to keep the Mac Garden as open and accessible as possible, we're happy to share that login support for Netscape 3.x is now live!
This means you can now log in directly from your 68k Macintosh to comment and even post in the forums. In fact, this very news post was written from a Mac IIci and NS3.

Our current theme still has limited support for older browsers, but we're exploring the idea of adding a second, more compatible theme in the future to improve the experience for everyone.

If you give it a try, we'd love to hear how it works for you from your real or emulated setup.
